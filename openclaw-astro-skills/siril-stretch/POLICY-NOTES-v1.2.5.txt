siril-stretch v1.2.5 policy refinement

What changes relative to v1.2.4:

1. Keep the successful v1.2.4 Round-2 calibrated profile family as the starting point.
2. Increase anti-overshoot scoring penalties once a candidate exceeds the preferred brightness neighborhood.
3. Prefer candidates with stronger normalized color contrast at slightly lower median luminance.
4. Add a softer-stop rule: if Round 2 is already strong, later rounds may continue only if they materially improve color contrast or structure without pushing median / p95 / p99.9 too high.
5. Final selection explicitly favors a slightly dimmer candidate when color separation is comparable.

Guidance neighborhood for the M16 calibration probe (advisory, not universal runtime hard targets):
- median: roughly 0.18 to 0.24
- p95: not much above ~0.36
- p99.9: not much above ~0.69 to 0.70
- saturation median: >= 0.26
- color_contrast_index: >= 0.31
- zero new clipping required

Round-2 profile emphasis:
- conservative / balanced / color-contrast profiles remain grounded in the v1.2.4 search family
- production scoring is biased slightly toward the darker side of that family
- continuation after Round 2 is discouraged unless later rounds improve color separation more than brightness
